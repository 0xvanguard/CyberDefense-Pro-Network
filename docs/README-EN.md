## 📌 What is CDPN?

**CyberDefense Pro Network** is a professional cybersecurity training platform designed for:

- 🎯 **Professionals** seeking certifications (OSCP, CEH, CISSP)
- 🎓 **Students** wanting to specialize in security
- 🏢 **SOC/Red Team teams** needing practical training
- 🌎 **Spanish speakers** looking for quality technical content

> *"We don't collect slides, we collect trained professionals."*
